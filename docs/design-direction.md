# CulturePilot design direction

Visual thesis: a midnight research studio with parchment typography, warm copper signals, and an expansive constellation that becomes a working evidence graph.

Content plan: the landing hero makes the promise; a three-part sequence explains taste, cross-category research, and evidence; a closing brief CTA leads into the research workspace. The application uses a narrow navigation rail, a large graph canvas, and a contextual evidence inspector.

Interaction thesis: progressive hero entrance, scroll reveals that explain the research chain, and spring-based brief/evidence transitions. Research animations follow streamed events. Reduced motion disables translation and animated edges.

Decorative constellation nodes describe research dimensions, not Qloo entities or results. No fictional findings, metrics, or reports appear in production.
